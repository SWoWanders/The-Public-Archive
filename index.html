The Public Archive

A card catalog for work that belongs to everyone — books, comics, newspapers, film, sound, art, ephemera, and games with no copyright left to clear. Everything here is free to view and, wherever the source allows it, free to download.

Sourced live from the Internet Archive, the Art Institute of Chicago, the Cleveland Museum of Art, the Library of Congress (incl. Chronicling America), and Wikimedia Commons.
Each item links back to its original record.
